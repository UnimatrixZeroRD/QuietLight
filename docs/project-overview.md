# Project Overview

## Name

**The Way of Quiet Light**

## Repository

**QuietLight**

## Purpose

This repository exists to build and maintain the official website for The Way of Quiet Light and its related creative works.

The website is not only an author page. It is intended to be a central spiritual and creative home for:

- The Way of Quiet Light.
- The Keeper's Lantern.
- The Book of Joshua.
- The Lantern Psalms.
- Devotional writings and reflections.
- Sacred music, hymns, psalms, and albums.
- Daily Light posts.
- Podcast, video, and social media links.
- Community support and project updates.

The website should help visitors understand what the Way of Quiet Light is, why it exists, and how they can read, listen, follow, support, and participate.

---

## Core Identity

The Way of Quiet Light is centered on stillness, humility, quiet service, reflection, and faithfulness.

The primary symbolic language is:

- **The lantern** — guarded light, carried gently.
- **The flame** — living faith, presence, and endurance.
- **The night** — trial, silence, uncertainty, and the place where light becomes visible.
- **The path** — a voluntary way of living, not a system of force.
- **The keeper** — one who tends the light without claiming to be the light.

Core phrase:

> I am not the light. I am only its keeper.

Core motto:

> The flame remains.

---

## What the Website Should Feel Like

The website should feel like entering a quiet chapel, a sacred library, and a cinematic devotional archive at the same time.

It should be:

- Reverent.
- Beautiful.
- Calm.
- Serious without being harsh.
- Spiritual without feeling cheap or gimmicky.
- Cinematic without becoming overwhelming.
- Premium without feeling corporate.
- Easy to navigate on desktop and mobile.

The site should not feel like a generic template. It should feel custom, atmospheric, and unmistakably connected to the visual world of the Quiet Light artwork.

---

## Main Audience

The expected audience includes:

- People interested in The Way of Quiet Light.
- Readers of The Keeper's Lantern.
- Listeners of the music and psalms.
- Followers from Facebook, YouTube, Spotify, Substack, and other platforms.
- People seeking devotional writing, scripture reflection, and sacred music.
- Supporters who want to follow or financially support the project.

---

## What the Website Is For

The site will act as the central hub for the project.

It should:

1. **Explain the Way**
   - What it is.
   - What it teaches.
   - What it is not.
   - How someone can begin reading or following along.

2. **Present the Book**
   - Introduce The Keeper's Lantern.
   - Explain the Book of Joshua.
   - Link to reading, ordering, or downloading options when available.

3. **Showcase the Music**
   - Feature albums and songs.
   - Link to Spotify, YouTube, and other music platforms.
   - Organize the sacred music catalog.

4. **Publish Devotional Content**
   - Daily Light posts.
   - Reflections.
   - Scripture-based writing.
   - Seasonal devotionals such as Good Friday, Easter, Advent, and Christmas.

5. **Build Trust and Continuity**
   - Give visitors a polished and coherent place to understand the project.
   - Keep the message organized.
   - Make the work feel established and cared for.

6. **Support Future Growth**
   - Newsletter.
   - Donations.
   - Book releases.
   - Music releases.
   - Community updates.

---

## What the Website Is Not

The site should avoid becoming:

- A cluttered social media feed.
- A noisy promotional landing page.
- A generic church website.
- A political commentary site.
- A scattered archive with no clear structure.
- A page overloaded with every image or every project at once.

Quiet Light has a strong visual identity. The website should preserve that identity by using the most powerful material selectively.

---

## Tone of Voice

The writing should be:

- Poetic but understandable.
- Reverent but welcoming.
- Strong but not aggressive.
- Personal but not self-centered.
- Spiritual but not vague.
- Clear enough for a new visitor.

Example tone:

> The Way of Quiet Light is a path of stillness, humility, and faithful reflection. It is not a path of noise or spectacle. It is the way of the lantern — carried gently, guarded faithfully, and offered to those still walking through the dark.

---

## Success Criteria

The first successful version of the website should:

- Look beautiful on desktop and mobile.
- Clearly explain the Way of Quiet Light within the first screen and first few sections.
- Feature The Keeper's Lantern prominently.
- Present the music catalog in a polished and organized way.
- Include a Daily Light or devotional section.
- Provide clear links to follow, listen, read, and support.
- Use the blue and gold visual system consistently.
- Be simple enough to maintain and expand.
