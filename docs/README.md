## Project Direction

The Quiet Light website should become the central home for:

- The Way of Quiet Light.
- The Keeper's Lantern.
- The Lantern Psalms.
- Sacred music and albums.
- Daily Light posts.
- Devotional writings.
- Joshua Eaton's Keeper persona and related creative work.

The design direction is:

> Deep blue stillness. Gold lantern light. Sacred reflection.
